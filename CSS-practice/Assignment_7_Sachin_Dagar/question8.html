Typography & Text Styling

A Short Article About Good Design

Good typography makes digital content easier to understand and more enjoyable to read.

Designers use font families, different font weights, and carefully chosen sizes to create hierarchy.

Readable text needs enough line-height, comfortable letter spacing, and appropriate text alignment. A balanced page guides the reader naturally from one idea to the next.
